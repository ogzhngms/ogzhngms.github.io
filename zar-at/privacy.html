Uygulama internete bağlanmaz ve internet izni istemez. Konum, kamera, mikrofon, rehber veya başka bir cihaz izni de istemez. Zar sonuçları cihazında üretilir ve hiçbir yere gönderilmez.

Reklam ve analitik

Zar At privacy policy

Zar At · Ogitech Studio · Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who is responsible?

Zar At is made by Ogitech Studio (Oğuzhan Gümüş). For privacy questions: oguzhan.gumus.0808@gmail.com.

What data do we collect?

None. Zar At does not collect, store or share personal data. There is no account or sign-in.

Internet and permissions

The app does not connect to the internet and does not request the internet permission. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other device permission. Dice results are generated on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Ads and analytics

We do not use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information kept on the device

The app keeps no permanent record on your device; your roll history is not saved. Small audio files for the dice sounds are written to a temporary folder and contain no personal information. Uninstalling the app removes everything that belongs to it.

Children

Because Zar At collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current text and date will be published on this page.
